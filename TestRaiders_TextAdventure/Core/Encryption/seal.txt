You enter the Ancient Seal Room.

The air is cold and unmoving. 
Strange runes glow faintly on the stone walls.

At the center of the room lies a stone altar.
Above it floats a shimmering crystal sphere—
the Heart of the Labyrinth.

As you touch it, a warm light surrounds you.
The darkness fades.
The ancient curse is broken.

Congratulations, adventurer.
You have completed your journey.
